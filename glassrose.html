01 Sinner's Secrets Field Note ▾

Recovered from: The Glass Rose, Slap Out. A bottle on the porch floor, still rolling; the stage mic left hot.

Subject: the narrator mid-confession — been thinking, been drinking, same problems riding "all the way into the ground."

Objects logged: a loaded bowl, a butane torch (clicked once at the end), a secret buried in the yard next to the car — roots grew up and found the bar.

On the record: "The secret is the glue. The secret is the rot. The only thing you have that the Law ain't got."

Status: asked Unc how deep they had to dig. Unc said deep enough to hit the Glitch. Narrator said he was already there.

02 Crank Field Note ▾

Recovered from: the chicken pit, a Friday night. A glass pipe rolling across a table; red lips walking the line at the end, smiling like everything was fine.

Subject: a woman called Crank. Trouble dressed up right. Said she used to be an angel before the fall, now likes watching good men crawl. (Cross-ref: Out of That Life — same name, found wrecked.)

On the record: "She makes the worst decisions feel like fate, and I keep saying yes anyway." "Some girls are poison, some girls are gasoline."

Also logged: Unc's warning — "some girls don't want saving, they just want company while they're self-destructing." Went unheeded.

Status: still wanted to ride. Bad for you and you know it.

03 The Room Still Knows Field Note ▾

Recovered from: a single-wide under a blood moon, rain on tin. A rain-soaked toy soldier in the muck; a wall clock stuck on the night.

Subject: a child in the corner, too awake to dream, cataloguing the room because nobody was watching the kid.

Objects logged: needles in a coffee can wrapped in a sack; a couch burning slow; a rotten jack-o-lantern under kerosene light; mason jar shine on the truck.

On the record: "Some houses don't stay behind you. Some ride in the blood." "You run from the devil till you run out of breath, then turn around and realize he was holding up a mirror."

Status: the child grew up wearing the room — one patch Outlaw, one patch Hurt. "I don't need no ghost now."

04 Blue Label Gods Field Note ▾

Recovered from: off Ann Street, a pump that barely worked. A Buick with the bumper held by wire, steaming by the tracks at sunrise; a lottery ticket taped to the mirror.

Subject: an all-night run for a sack that kept being one county east. "Tonight we find the sack or we don't come back."

Logged along the route: a bartender taking tips on EBT; Cricket selling dreams from a tackle box; a lead east to "a man named Moses — not that Moses, the one from Prattville. Drives a primer-gray Camaro with no reverse gear."

Also present: Wanda, scratch-off crown, laid three cards — one jail, one rain, one a little white bag with a hole burned through it. "Pick one, queen. They all end the same."

Status: walked east at sunrise — one boot on, one boot gone, Blue Label empty — saying "I know it's close."

05 The Burnt Out Preacher Field Note ▾

Recovered from: past Montgomery, where the dead roads are. A wet choir robe on a fence wire, a duct-taped Bible under the light, Blue Label glass in a gravel fire.

Subject: a burnt out preacher in gas-station shades and a torn choir robe; said he'd show the way to the flood; gone by sunrise.

Logged along the route: a woman with baby teeth in a pill bottle and a motel key from room thirteen; a man named Mercy asleep under the bridge; a girl in white boots — "I keep leaving but I ain't left yet."

On the record: "You're just a witness, counting ghosts that still got names." "The flood don't come with trumpets loud. Sometimes it comes as a county road, washed out."

Status: the floodlight kept blinking — on, off, on, off — like heaven had trouble making up its mind.

06 Waste Some Time Field Note ▾

Recovered from: a porch, a rocking chair creaking. A flatbed Ford looking at Copperhead Dam; a jar getting passed.

Subject: told to focus, build a career; said his career was surviving the next ten minutes; chose the porch.

Anomalies, reported without alarm: the sun is just a lightbulb; the woods are made of glass; the world going white.

Also present: the Hat Man — no longer at the threshold, just on the porch, wasting time alongside.

Status: clock's broken anyway. Unc lost his watch in 2010. Nobody's keeping time out here.

07 Through That Cut Field Note ▾

Recovered from: an extended stay past the interstate; a trail through the weeds and broken brick to the lot. The air felt wrong before anything happened.

Subject: a couple zips of clear, getting late; a room humming like a live-wire bed, weight open on the table.

Also logged: they stopped Cornbread, searched him down, boxed the lot — he threw it before they got him. Still in jail.

On the record: "Ain't even the room. It's that walk back. Through that cut. Knowing if something moves wrong, you wearing all of it."

Status: same little trail, different night. That walk back got a hell of a lot longer.

08 Slap Out Rules Field Note ▾

Recovered from: a backroad past the county line. An old Chevy coughing, milk going warm in the backseat, a church sign half gone reading THE FLOOD IS COMING with the letters leaning wrong.

Subject: slap out — of gas, cash, sleep, mercy.

Also logged: a man named Deacon in a Dale Earnhardt shirt, torch in the cupholder — said he wasn't a dealer, he was "a blessing with legs," then shorted the sack and quoted Leviticus wrong.

On the record: "When you slap out, you start naming crumbs miracles. A half bag becomes manna. Every liar with supply sounds like Moses for a minute." "The store became wilderness, the plug became law."

Status: pulled in quiet with the day gone gray. You find out quick what god you serve when the last crumb falls.

09 The Map's a Circle Field Note ▾

Recovered from: behind the Dollar General, a head full of thunder; the air felt bent, like a bad streetlight; a rattling pill bottle, one shake, at the end.

Subject: woke up already wrong, kept moving, walked the river-walk road deeper than he meant to.

Logged along the route: a Texaco man with three gold teeth selling a meeting with God; a meth witch where the dead weeds grow; a church bus behind the Waffle House, NO BRAKES on the side, twelve little men in tuxedos singing Amazing Grace backwards.

Arrived at: the Flesh Carnival — a pop-up tent city behind a Chuck E. Cheese wall; neon crucifixes; a sad animatronic raccoon nodding like it knew every bad decision in every room. Wanda dancing slow on cardboard.

Status: "The map's a circle, baby. It always brings you back." The choice got made a long time before him.

10 Neon Judgment Field Note ▾

Recovered from: a motel strip, wet asphalt, poisoned night air. The crickets had quit; the sky was bleeding color.

Subject: walked where the ash met the asphalt, reading the end in the signs; the sky a screen, the signal crossed.

Logged along the strip: a motel priest preaching dice-roll sermons; a corner boy selling "apocalypse in a baggie of blue"; a girl named Grace selling pills and fate; a man with a crow on each shoulder; the Horsemen, riding where the debt gets rolled.

On the record: "The sky is a screen and the signal got crossed." "The prophets wear hoodies and the lost read lines."

Status: the roof came gone. Riding the lightning till dawn. Horsemen in the rearview now. "Let them come."

11 Gravel and Grace Field Note ▾

Recovered from: deep where the road turns clay, pines leaning like they know the name. Rain on a Chevrolet roof; a bowl gone cold as shame.

Subject (the seat): ten years burned for a dirty high; a child somewhere with the same eyes; a woman who quit waiting when the headlights kept turning south.

Logged at the edge: pulled off deep, thought about letting the ditch keep what was left. No angel came. No reveal. Just one more breath, unasked for.

On the record: "The devil don't need to kill you if he can make you agree with him." "Pain ain't proof that you're beyond grace." "Forgiven ain't a feeling, son. Drive."

Status: drove on. Not clean, not free. The engine turned over rough.

12 The Last Bowl Field Note ▾

Recovered from: a single-wide, rain since Tuesday.

Subject (the seat): three generations collapsing to one point — granddaddy's ring in a Montgomery pawn shop, the daughter who don't know his name, mama's ghost in the corner, daddy's in the mirror.

Anomalies: the dead present in the room; the devil wearing kin's face; rain turning to blood.

On the record: "The fall ain't a moment — it's a lifetime of fallin'." "The devil don't need your soul. He just needs you to keep believin' you can outrun the hole." "The Glitch is open."

Status: one more click of the torch. Event horizon. The bowl goes out for good.

13 Montgomery Mercy Field Note ▾

Recovered from: a Montgomery motel after midnight. Rain on a busted AC unit; room twelve coughing through a broken vent; red clay mud on the boots.

Subject: a woman who said her name was Mercy once, but nobody calls her that these days; motel bands on her wrist; checking an empty pocket like her whole life should be in it.

Also present: Worm reading Revelation; June crying in the bathroom; Tate pacing with a tire iron. The before: "I wore white on Easter morning — before the pipe, before the kerosene."

On the record: "You call it a weekend, the Devil calls it inventory."

Status: said pull over by the ditch — "if I keep riding with you, I might mistake your road for mine." Got out. "If I call collect… don't answer like you know me."

14 How to Keep What Won't Leave Field Note ▾

Recovered from: the dirt road west, looking for something clean. Sun dropping, dust hanging gold; past the bait shop, the church with no sign, the field where the hay rolled black.

Subject: chasing an answer down a dead-end sky; every voice gave a fragment, every mile took a piece.

Logged along the road: a man on an ice chest, pill bottle and Bible — "the trick ain't seeing deeper, it's knowing which lie you can survive"; a barefoot woman by a soybean ditch — "what if all it owes is a seat in the fire."

At the river: Unc at a burn barrel — "water don't get free, it just accepts the bank and keeps moving anyway." "Acceptance ain't surrender — it's the end of arguing with what is."

Status: drove back slow. Didn't feel saved. Just quieter. "Maybe the whole point was never getting out — just learning how to stay without lying to myself."
